Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our safety protocols, warranties, and treatment process.

Is your pest control treatment safe for kids and pets? +
Yes. We utilize low-odor biological enzyme flushes and government-approved Bayer synthetic pyrethroids and bait gels. Formulated to minimize airborne fumes, our treatments are safe for families, infants, and pets when applied as directed by our certified technicians.
Do I need to empty my kitchen cabinets before cockroach control? +
No! Our odorless Bayer Maxforce Gel Treatment does NOT require emptying utensils, containers, or groceries. Our technician strategically applies pin-sized dots inside cabinet hinges, behind refrigerators, and under sinks where cockroaches harbor.
How long does a standard treatment session take? +
A typical residential 2 BHK or 3 BHK apartment takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes. Commercial kitchen and termite radar treatments take 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the carpet area and severity of infestation.
What happens if pests reappear during the warranty period? +
All our treatments come with a written warranty (from 1 Year up to 5 Years depending on the package). If you notice any pest activity within the warranty period, we provide complimentary re-inspection and re-treatment in accordance with warranty terms.
Do you provide audit certificates for restaurants and food businesses? +
Yes. For all commercial clients, we provide complete digital Integrated Pest Management (IPM) logbooks, bait station barcode audits, chemical MSDS safety sheets, and FSSAI/HACCP compliance clearance certificates.
